# Project requirements

All design, content, implementation, and future changes must support Google SEO, Google Business Profile discoverability, and AI search throughout this project.

- Use Thai-first, useful, crawlable server-rendered content, semantic HTML, clear heading hierarchy, descriptive internal links, and accessible images.
- Provide unique page titles and descriptions, canonical URLs when the real production domain is configured, sitemap, robots, and appropriate truthful JSON-LD.
- Keep company identity and contact information in one central configuration. Keep the verified name, address, phone, opening hours, website, and Google Business Profile consistent when supplied.
- Do not invent business locations, certifications, ratings, reviews, years, statistics, prices, product specifications, or customer projects. Do not emit placeholder contacts or demo projects as real structured data.
- Support Organization, BreadcrumbList, and appropriate product/article structured data only when their required factual fields are available. Never add fake review, offer, or local-business data to qualify for rich results.
- Write direct, factual answers to real customer questions. Provide clear product categories, applications, service scope, and attributable company information for search engines and AI retrieval.
- Optimize responsive performance, image dimensions, loading, readability, and Core Web Vitals. Theme changes must preserve semantic content and accessibility.
- Google Business Profile verification and external publishing require actual company information and user authorization. Search rankings and AI citations cannot be guaranteed.
- Keep demonstration inquiry handling explicit even in presentation mode. Do not claim submissions have reached the company without a backend.
- Before launch, verify production domain, indexing configuration, sitemap, metadata, contact consistency, and structured data. Local demonstration must not publish a fabricated canonical domain.

Continue the full original website scope: all routes, two persistent themes, supplied assets, responsive layouts, accessible interactions, and local preview.
